# SolveSphere AI — Student Guide

## Sign in
Register or log in with the institution-approved account and ensure the correct academic details are associated with your profile. Do not share login credentials.

## Institutional Enrollment Verification
- Upon registration or signing in with Google, students enter their official institutional enrollment number, department, and semester on `/auth/complete-profile`.
- Enrollment numbers require administrative verification against official university records before protected features (such as maintenance complaint dispatch and continuous assessment quizzes) unlock.
- While verification is `PENDING`, you may browse general course outlines and club directories in restricted mode.
- If your submission is `REJECTED`, the administrator's official explanation is displayed on `/auth/complete-profile` with an option to correct your roll number and resubmit.

## Academic
- `/portal/subjects`: browse courses that the institution has actually published.
- `/portal/timeline`: view current syllabus topic statuses.
- `/portal/resources`: access faculty-provided resources.
- `/portal/practice`: take published quizzes. Results are calculated by the trusted backend only; missing or unavailable quizzes must not yield simulated scores.
- `/portal/studymate`: use academic AI help. Verify substantive answers against approved course materials.

## Campus
- `/portal/report`: submit an actual maintenance complaint with an accurate location/category and optional allowed attachment.
- `/portal/tickets`: view your own tickets, current status and audit history; request reopening only where the server permits it.
- `/portal/campus-care`: get campus workflow advice and an optional AI-generated complaint draft. Suggestions never automatically submit or resolve tickets.
- `/portal/campus`: view verified public incidents only.
- `/portal/clubs`: browse clubs registered by administrators. An on-page membership demo is disabled; contact the verified club lead for joining instructions.

## If a page is empty
An empty list may mean the institution has not entered or published real records yet. Do not assume sample subjects, imaginary incidents, or practice grades are official. Ask authorized faculty or administrators when content will be available.

## Privacy
Students may access only authorized private records. Do not attach confidential third-party data. The Speak Freely sensitive-submission channel is currently paused pending a verified privacy design.
